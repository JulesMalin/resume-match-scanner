The 7-point checklist

Everything above the scanner can't see from pasted text. Tick all seven before you apply.

  • Optimal format, layout, and section order for your experience level.
  • No spelling or grammar errors. Run it through Grammarly.
  • Every link works: LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio.
  • No confusing jargon or unexplained abbreviations.
  • One page.
  • Aligned to the specific job description you're applying for.
  • Saved and submitted as a PDF.

Which sections to include

Space is scarce. Keep what earns interviews, cut what doesn't.

Required
  • Contact information
  • Professional experience
  • Education (with GPA)
  • Technical skills
Optional
  • Experience summary
  • Projects
  • Certifications
  • Publications
Avoid
  • Interests
  • Hobbies
  • Volunteer experience
  • Side hustles
